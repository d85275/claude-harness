# CH-28: Pre-publish privacy sweep of the whole history

## Status

planned (2026-11-23), Milestone C (release/publishing)

## Depends on

CH-27

## Scope

Before the repo goes public, check every commit, not only the current tree.

- Scan all of history (`git log -p --all`, every blob) for: the private project names and paths (from the coordinator's brief, never written into tracked files), real ticket IDs and prefixes, personal paths (`/Users/`), emails other than the owner's kept commit email (owner decision 2026-11-22), tokens and keys (common secret patterns), and screenshots or files copied from private projects.
- Also the current tree: remove or generalise anything found (e.g. `PLAN-0.3.0.md` names both private projects and should be deleted now that its work is in tickets).
- Report every hit in history with commit and file, grouped, to the owner. Rewriting history is the owner's decision (a boundary); this ticket does not rewrite it.

Don't: rewrite history, push, tag or change visibility.

## Done criteria

- A report of history hits (in the scratchpad, summarised without the private terms in the ticket file).
- The current tree has zero hits (scan rerun after the fix).
- Owner decision recorded: rewrite history, or accept the listed hits. Review rounds: at most 2.

## Allowed files

any tracked file (removals and generalisations only), `docs/tickets/CH-28.md`

## Review level

high-risk (release/publishing)
